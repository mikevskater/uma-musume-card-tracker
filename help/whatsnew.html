Version 1.8.1

September 14, 2026
Bug Fix
Deck Finder: “included cards” are now actually included

When you used the Deck Finder’s include-card filters (“all must be in the deck” or “at least one”), the results could silently ignore the filter — and would only select included cards if scored high (GitHub issue #7).

The finder builds early candidate decks to speed up the search, and those candidates were being shown as results without being checked against your include filters. Candidates that broke the rules scored higher than correct decks and pushed the valid ones out of your results.

Candidate decks are now validated against your include filters before they can appear as results, and every deck is always exactly 6 cards. If your selected cards can’t fit the type composition you picked (for example, including an Wit card while your composition has no Wit slots), the finder tells you that directly instead of returning empty or incorrect results.

Version 1.8.0

September 13, 2026
Major
Conditional unique effects - non simulation needed effects

42 SSRs carry conditional unique effects — bonuses that only kick in under certain conditions. The training simulator previously ignored all of them, so projections for those decks were a floor. Six cards now have their effect fully calculated:

  • Agnes Digital “A Fan's Joy” — +15% training effectiveness when the deck has 5 distinct card types.
  • Satono Diamond “Special Dreamers!” — +10% training effectiveness with 4 or more distinct card types.
  • Symboli Rudolf “Enchaïnement” & Daitaku Helios “Sunlit Angel ♪” — +10 to every initial stat per 2 distinct card types in the deck. *Provisional — the scaling formula was inferred from game data; community guides couldn't be consulted, so the exact step size is unverified.*
  • Maruzensky “Sentimental Flare ♪” — +8% training effectiveness when the facility is level 5.
  • Light Hello “From the Ground Up” — −28% energy cost while friendship training is happening. *Provisional — in the game the effect is gated on the friendship gauge; the builder has no gauge, so the reduction is assumed active whenever the friendship-training toggle is on.*

Conditionals on these cards are now included in projected gains, energy costs, initial stats, and the per-training breakdown. In the deck summary, every unique effect is tagged calculated (green) or display only (amber) so you can see at a glance which bonuses the simulator accounts for.

Effect parameters were decoded from the game's master data (the unique-effect parameter tables plus the in-game English descriptions). Where the numbers match the written description they're solid; where they had to be inferred they're marked provisional above.

Bug Fix
Deck Finder no longer scores conditional effects

Conditional unique effects store their conditions as raw parameters — a gauge threshold of 80, a fan target of 10,000. Those values were leaking into the Deck Finder's effect-sum tie-breaker, silently inflating cards like Taiki Shuttle and Narita Top Road in close comparisons. They are now excluded from finder scoring and from the effect tables; conditional effects are display only in the finder for now.

What is still not modeled

33 cards keep display-only conditional effects. They depend on per-run state (friendship gauge, energy, fans, failures) that a static deck projection can't evaluate — treat numbers for decks using them as a floor:

  • Stat bonus scaled by the friendship gauge (24 cards): Taiki Shuttle, Heirs to the Throne, Team Sirius, Nishino Flower, Mejiro Bright, Marvelous Sunday, Air Shakur, Daiwa Scarlet, Mr. C.B., Rice Shower, Agnes Tachyon, Matikanetannhauser, Zenno Rob Roy, Air Groove, Sakura Chiyono O, Manhattan Cafe, Biwa Hayahide, Daiichi Ruby, Shinko Windy, Symboli Kris S, Mejiro Ardan, Yaeno Muteki, Mihono Bourbon, Tanino Gimlet
  • Training eff. on non-preferred training: Sakura Bakushin O
  • Training eff. by fans gained: Narita Top Road
  • Stacking friendship bonus: Sirius Symboli, Twin Turbo
  • Training eff. from deck friendship: Ikuno Dictus, Tokai Teio
  • Training eff. by cards trained with: El Condor Pasa
  • Failure rate reduction: Nakayama Festa
  • Initial friendship gauge: Oguri Cap

3 cards need an energy model before they can be calculated (Bamboo Memory, Seeking the Pearl, Mejiro Palmer — their bonuses scale with how much energy is left). A decision on how to model energy over a full run is pending — until then their numbers are also a floor.

Deck comparisons within the same scenario remain meaningful, since every deck is missing the same bonuses. The remaining effects are planned for the full scenario-simulator project.

🎤 Grand Concert scenario support

The new Brighter Together: Our Grand Concert scenario (released 07/22) is now selectable in both the Deck Builder and the Deck Finder, using the training values pulled straight from the game.

  • Accurate training values — all five facilities across levels 1–5, including Grand Concert's distinctive Wit training that gains energy.
  • Higher stat caps — the scenario raises caps by +400 Speed, +300 Guts, and +100 each to Stamina, Power, and Wit.
  • Strategy tips for the scenario added to the Deck Builder summary panel.
Feature
Deck Finder scoring tuned for Grand Concert

Grand Concert inverts the usual priorities, so it gets its own scoring profile rather than borrowing URA's:

  • Race Bonus is heavily deprioritized. Races award no Performance Points, so optional racing is discouraged — Race Bonus drops to the lowest weight of any scenario, and its diminishing-returns point falls from 34% to 20%.
  • Skill Point Bonus is promoted sharply. Skipping optional races leaves runs short on skill points, so the Stat Bonus weight (which includes Skill Point Bonus) is now more than double its URA value.
  • Hint weights raised — hint levels reduce what a skill costs to buy, which matters far more when skill points are scarce.
  • Training Effectiveness and Friendship Bonus lead, with Initial Friendship weighted higher than in any other scenario to get Friendship Training online sooner.

Weights were set from the community consensus in the game8 and uma.guide Grand Concert guides. As always, you can override any of them in the Scoring Weights panel — and if the meta settles somewhere else, please say so.

What Grand Concert support does not cover yet

The training simulator models Grand Concert's base training values and stat caps, but not its scenario-specific progression loop:

  • Treat Grand Concert numbers in the Deck Builder as a floor — a real run will land higher once Concert bonuses accumulate.
  • Deck comparisons within Grand Concert are still meaningful, since every deck is missing the same bonuses.

Version 1.6.2

July 19, 2026
Bug Fix
Late Surger and End Closer aptitudes were swapped

Thanks to everyone who reported this. Trainee running style aptitudes were displaying under the wrong labels — a trainee's Late Surger grade was shown as End Closer and vice versa. This affected the trainee picker, the deck summary, and the Build Focus dropdown.

  • All three now match the grades shown in game. For example, Special Week correctly shows Late Surger A / End Closer C.
  • Your saved decks and Build Focus selections are unaffected — only the labels were wrong, not the stored data.
Bug Fix
Deck Finder scored running style skills against the wrong aptitude

The same mix-up affected how the Deck Finder weighs a card's hint skills against your trainee's aptitudes:

  • Late Surger skills were being scored against the trainee's End Closer aptitude.
  • End Closer skills were not being scored at all — they always counted as zero aptitude, no matter which trainee was selected.

If you previously ran searches with a Late Surger or End Closer trainee, it's worth running them again — the recommended cards may change.
